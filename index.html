OUR STORY / 12

WE DON’T WANT YOU TO
THROW THE STORY AWAY.

Still Yours started with a simple thought: why do we treat meaningful things as disposable just because they are no longer new?

Our vision is to bring emotion back into fashion by combining sustainability, technology and personalization.

We don’t want people to simply buy products. We want them to create something they can remember.

OUR VISION

“To create a future where fashion isn’t disposable, memories aren’t forgotten and every product has the possibility of a second story.”

OUR MISSION

“To combine fashion, AI and emotional storytelling to help people rewear, personalize and reconnect with the things they love.”
